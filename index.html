The boring competitor

Alzheimer's EEG already slows — and this dataset shows it clearly.

Peak alpha frequency was lower in AD by about 1.18 Hz on average, theta/alpha was higher, and alpha relative power was lower. Those are exactly the kinds of conventional effects Φ-Dwell must beat or complement.

Confirmed in this cohort

Spectral slowing

The simple baseline is not a straw man. It is visibly strong.

Still interesting

Spatial dwell

Despite that baseline, adding dwell gradient increased mean internal CV AUC from 0.729 to 0.768.

Because dwell gradient was invented after inspecting ds004504, the +0.039 cannot be treated as an unbiased effect size. Cross-validation cannot undo feature-selection history.

New warning

Age matters more than the old code admitted.

The legacy parser missed the capitalized Age field. Once fixed in the audit, dwell gradient showed a strong relationship with age among controls but not among AD subjects.

Dwell CV is legitimate; that threshold alone does not prove a critical phase transition.

Descriptive only

Alzheimer perplexity

Quarantine

PSI / Gerchberg–Saxton

Real algorithm, unvalidated biological interpretation. The old “p=.006 survives ≈.003 Bonferroni” claim is arithmetically false.
