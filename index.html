The boring competitor

Alzheimer's EEG already slows. Φ-Dwell has to add something beyond that.

A 2025 systematic review of 70 biomarker-proven dementia EEG studies found spectral slowing to be a common observation across dementias and described EEG as a promising low-cost, accessible modality. Other recent work reports increased theta-related power and theta/alpha or theta/beta ratios in AD, while alpha-band connectivity changes are among the most recurrent network findings.

That creates an obvious alternative explanation for the Φ-Dwell result: shorter or altered alpha-mode dwell may simply be another representation of ordinary spectral slowing.

Reality audit

Five things that materially change the old interpretation

Repair

Categorical Pearson coupling

Dominant mode IDs are labels. Pearson correlation changes under arbitrary relabeling, so the current cross-band coupling measure is mathematically inappropriate. Use label-invariant mutual information or continuous coefficient vectors.

Rename

“Criticality” from CV > 1

Dwell-time CV is legitimate. A threshold at one does not establish a critical phase transition. Call it dwell variability unless stronger evidence is added.

Confounded

Task vocabulary doubling

Historical REST and TASK had very different amounts of data, and whole imagery recordings were labeled TASK despite alternating T0 and T1/T2 epochs. Re-run equal-duration event-conditioned comparisons.

Descriptive only

Alzheimer bigram perplexity

The legacy analyzer builds and scores the bigram model on the same sequence. It is training perplexity, not held-out prediction.

Bug

Age was not parsed

The public table uses Age; the old parser reads lowercase age. Saved results contain age: null, so old outputs are not age-adjusted.

Quarantine

PSI / Gerchberg–Saxton

A real mathematical algorithm applied through an invented radial canvas. Interesting probe, but its biological meaning is not independently established. The historical claim that p=.006 survives a ≈.003 Bonferroni threshold is also arithmetically false.

Severity versus diagnosis

The pooled MMSE correlation is not the severity test we want.

In ds004504 every control participant has MMSE 30, while AD and FTD participants have lower and variable scores. A feature that separates diagnoses will therefore tend to correlate with pooled MMSE even if it does not track severity within Alzheimer's disease.

Historical

Pool everybody

Useful as a descriptive association, but diagnosis is baked into the MMSE distribution.

Audit

Within AD

Correlate the frozen dwell gradient with MMSE among AD subjects only. This asks whether the feature tracks severity rather than merely category.

Context

Why spectral slowing is the mandatory baseline

  • Bruffaerts et al. (2025), diagnostic utility of electrophysiological markers in AD/FTD/DLB — systematic review of 70 biomarker-proven studies; spectral slowing was a common observation across diseases.
  • EEG biomarkers in Alzheimer's and prodromal Alzheimer's — reports significant theta/alpha and theta/beta power-ratio differences and increased theta power in AD.
  • Paitel et al. (2025), systematic review of EEG connectivity in AD/MCI — alpha-band connectivity effects were especially recurrent, while the literature remained methodologically heterogeneous.
  • OpenNeuro ds004504 — the 88-subject discovery dataset used by the historical Alzheimer's branch.

Clinical boundary: research software only. No prospective diagnostic accuracy, clinical utility, or generalization across acquisition systems has been established.
